Tower defence game
Squares, circles and triangles are the defence systems.
They have multiple shapes inside of them when upgraded (square in a square in a square. Square in a triangle..etc)
They shoot their relative shapes (they spin when they shoot - when a target is in range)
The enemies are dots of various sizes (random) and their size determines their health, which gets smaller as they’re hit, until they vanish.
As the waves increase, land is removed, making it harder to build defence systems.
The map begins with an single path weaving through. Think of the grand canyon, I want a path to randomly generate from top to bottom. 

Enemies are spawned from the top. They find the path and walk down at a set pace.

The user can spend money to buy or upgrade units. Units can have 10 levels each, each of those have a different colour.
There are different types of units, square, circle and triangle.
You can upgrade a unit by adding a square to a circle or a circle to a square.

The different types of units are, lets say one is a long range attack (triangle)
circle is an area effect weapon, like a flamethrower that shoots bursts, flame on, flame off, flame on, flame off.
Squares can be a mid range machine gun.

But if you add a triangle to a square, you get longer range machine gun: square first, triangle second.
Or if you add a square to a triangle, you get a faster firing long range weapon.
When you upgrade a unit, you upgrade the whole thing and you can stack 1 of each, so circle, square, triangle on each. Each of those is a new level, so if you have a square, triangle and circle, it's lvl 3, you can then upgrade up to lvl 10 for more speed, more range, more damage after that.
